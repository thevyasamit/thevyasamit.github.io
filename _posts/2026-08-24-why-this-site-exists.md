We live in a digital and perhaps AI-first world now, and I believe this site can be useful as my digital address, something that can be useful both for humans and AI agents to understand my work, philosophies, and ideas.

I keep updating this section, sometimes just asking AI to make changes to what I write with a lot of typos, and sometimes it's me writing the whole thing.

I think GitHub has done a great job, and I want to leverage this as a way NOT just to show my resume, work, etc., but also to write about what I think about, or perhaps how my work, interactions, and observations are shaping my thoughts.

## What I expect to write about

Roughly three things, which is also roughly what I spend my days on:

- **AI Technology.** I work in tech, and since a lot of tech these days is AI, so it may...
- **Engineering.** I've built things in the backend, and engineering backend systems is always fun.
- **Philosophy/Personal observations.**  I like to know how things work, not engineering-wise but the cause, the motivation behind them, and also to understand the reasoning. It all starts with simple observations.

## How this works

Posts are plain Markdown files in `_posts/`. The site is Jekyll on GitHub
Pages, so publishing is a `git push` — no CMS, no database, nothing to keep
patched. Everything is filterable by tag and searchable from the
[writing index](/writing/).

No newsletter, no popups, no tracking. Just the words.
