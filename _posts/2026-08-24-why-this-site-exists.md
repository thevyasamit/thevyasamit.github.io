We are in a world where almost everything is virtual. In the earlier days of
civilisation people had a home address; I think now is the time to also have an
address on the web — somewhere that is yours, that outlives whichever platform
is fashionable this year.

So this is mine. It has been sitting here for a while as a static résumé. I'm
turning it into something I actually write on.

## What I expect to write about

Roughly three things, which is also roughly what I spend my days on:

- **AI, minus the hype.** I work at an AI institute, which mostly means I see
  how much unglamorous infrastructure sits underneath every impressive demo.
  I'd like to write about that gap.
- **Engineering.** APIs, Kubernetes, the parts of Python that surprise me, and
  documentation as a first-class artifact rather than an afterthought.
- **Health and everything else.** Occasionally. It's my site.

## How this works

Posts are plain Markdown files in `_posts/`. The site is Jekyll on GitHub
Pages, so publishing is a `git push` — no CMS, no database, nothing to keep
patched. Everything is filterable by tag and searchable from the
[writing index](/writing/).

No newsletter, no popups, no tracking. Just the words.
